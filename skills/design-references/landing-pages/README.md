# Landing page references

Landing pages Hasan likes. Use them as inspiration, not templates.

## Petal (https://petal.bar/)

`petal-bar.png` is a full-page screenshot from 2026-09-30. The blank area under the hero is a scroll animation that the headless capture didn't render, so open the live site to see it.

- Small Mac menu bar app, sold for $3.99 once. The page is short: nav, hero, product shot, one-line pitch, a multi-account feature section, FAQ, a repeat CTA, and the footer.
- The hero is a big, tight, bold sans-serif headline ("Know how much AI you have left.") with a hand-drawn underline on the key words, one grey sentence under it, a black pill button, and the price in small grey text.
- The page is white with lots of whitespace. The real product UI sits inside soft grey rounded panels and does most of the selling.
- The pitch sentence mixes dark and grey text and puts service logos inline in the words. Small colored petal shapes float at the edges as decoration.
- Copy is short and plain, with one idea per section.
