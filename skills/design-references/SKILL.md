Hasan likes these designs. Use them as inspiration for the current task, not as templates to copy. Take what fits and still make something that suits this project.

- `landing-pages/` holds landing pages.

Open the folder that matches the task, read its README, and look at its images or contact sheets before you start.
- `logos/` holds ready-to-use brand SVGs (Cloudflare, AWS, DigitalOcean, Google, Anthropic, OpenAI, Convex, PlanetScale, MongoDB, 1Password, OpenRouter, Vercel, GitHub, Hostinger, Zoho, ElevenLabs, GoDaddy, X). Reuse them before fetching from svgl.app, and add new ones here after you fetch them. Simple Icons (`https://cdn.simpleicons.org/<name>`) covers brands svgl lacks; svgl needs a browser User-Agent header.

Use `$squint` when building or reviewing the design. Diagram references and creation instructions now live in `$diagrams`.
