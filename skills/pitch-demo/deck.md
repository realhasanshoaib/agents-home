# The proposal deck

The deck makes one argument, in the order the buyer decides: what changes for them, the proof, what is real and what is proposed, and the one next step. Its words come from the brief, so a builder lays them out and doesn't write new claims. Legend's deck, `/Users/macmini/code/legend-autohub-demo/public/proposal/index.html`, is a visual shell to reuse (brands, full bleed, embeds), not a running order.

## Slides

| Slide | Brand | Who gets it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| What changes for them, in one sentence | Both | Everyone |
| Their pain, in their words or our proof, with the source | Theirs | Everyone |
| The demo, opened at the hard moment from the brief | Theirs | Everyone |
| What works today and what we would build | Theirs | Everyone |
| One or two projects that prove we've done this | Ours | Everyone |
| Scope and the time from signing to go-live | Theirs | After a meeting |
| Commercials | Ours | After a meeting |
| What we need from you | Theirs | After a meeting |
| The next step | Both | Everyone |

A cold prospect's deck has at most 6 slides, and a warm one at most 9. Add a slide only when the brief shows the buyer needs it, such as a "What Ava is worth" slide when the numbers back it.

## Look

- **Two brands.** Their slides use their colours, font and logo, and ours use Q9's paper and ink. Every slide carries a logo pair at the foot: "<Prospect> × Q9 Labs" on theirs and "Q9 Labs × <Prospect>" on ours. There is no light and dark switch, because each slide takes its brand's colours.
- **Full bleed.** Each slide fills the window at any shape, with no bars above, below or beside it.
- **Visuals over text.** Every screenshot shows the product while it works, never a test screen or a marketing home page. Use an icon beside each fact, real logos, photos of their real products, and our agent's face. Group things with spacing and soft filled panels. Hasan dislikes thin lines between rows and accent lines over columns.
- **Whole pictures.** Show every photo at its natural proportions, never cut to fill a fixed strip. On a phone, the first screen shows the promise before any photo.

## Demos on the slides

Each demo plays on its slide and loads only when its slide comes up. Make the proof big enough to read at 390 px: show the moment that matters at full width, and keep frames for when the frame itself helps. Next to each demo, a labelled button such as "Open the live site" opens it alone, and one plain line says it is live and can be clicked. A small corner icon alone is too easy to miss. Every picture of the product shows the current build.

## Proof

Show the one or two projects closest to what this buyer worries about, each with a real screenshot and an outcome. For a school, feature Edli (LMS-School), our main traction product. Name clients only as Q9 Brain Fact `q9-deck-client-naming` allows, and describe the rest by industry and country with an icon.

## Commercials and the next step

- **Commercials.** Give the setup fee, paid in full upfront on signing and never split, and the monthly fee, under one "Tentative, depends on the final scope" note. Leave out the first-year total.
- **The next step.** The last slide asks for the brief's one dated next step, the same one the email asks for, with the name of the person who answers it. Show both logos. Hasan's name stays off it.

## Before it goes

The deck is unlisted, with `noindex`, a preview card, and a print layout so they can save it as a PDF. Done when contact sheets show every slide at 1440 × 900, a 16:10 window, a tall window and 390 px wide, with every picture whole, and every embed works on a phone. Start one live call from inside the deck on a Mac with a microphone, because a headless browser can't.
